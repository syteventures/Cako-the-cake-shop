Good to know

  • Everything at Cako is vegetarian.
  • Several cakes have almonds or nut crunch. Tell us about any allergy when you order.
  • For anything about ingredients, including egg, ask us on WhatsApp.
  • Not every cake is on the counter every day. Ask us what’s ready today.

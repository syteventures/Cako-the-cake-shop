## Scope

Surface: index.html, the one-page site for Cako The Cake Shop (demo pitch). Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: people near Upper Kondhwa Road, Bibvewadi, Pune, mostly on phones, often in the evening, sorting a cake for today or a birthday that starts at midnight. Action: send a cake order on WhatsApp (pre-filled) or call; secondary: get directions, order on Zomato.
Proof on hand: hours 11 am to 12 midnight daily (binding), address, phone, vegetarian only, delivery, takeaway, seating, about Rs 100 for one. Demo-only: menu prices, reviews (client-requested), stand-in photographs from Wikimedia Commons.
Constraints: no unconfirmed claims stated as fact; demo content listed for replacement; reviews never in structured data; static HTML/CSS/JS; light on a mid-range Android.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Cako is the cake shop still open when a birthday starts at midnight. The page is that night: it reads the real Pune clock, shows how long the counter stays open, and lets you write the name on the cake and send the order before twelve. It refuses the category default: pastel bakery hero, a "baked with love" line, a grid of identical product cards, a testimonial carousel.

OWN-WORLD: Night ground of dark chocolate, frosting-cream type, Cako red #EF3334 only on the badge and on actions, candle amber only for the live time and the now mark. One bright island: the counter, lit cream like a glass showcase, edged with the logo's scallop as a piped border. Shrikhand headlines, Mukta text, piped gel lettering only on the cake. Flat fills; light lives in the photographs.

STORY: The visitor sees Cako is open tonight and until when, believes it can still save the birthday, writes the name, picks cake and size, sends the WhatsApp order or calls; then checks the counter, reads neighbours, finds the address.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed candlelit cake photograph is the table. Over its dark side: the h1 at display size, the live line in amber, and the order slip on cream paper with a top-down cake whose piped border is the logo's scallop and whose surface shows the typed name; cake, size, when; red Send order on WhatsApp plus Call. The hours line, 11 am to 12 midnight with now lit, runs along the bottom edge as the table's edge. Phone: photo band on top, h1 over its foot, slip below, fixed action bar.

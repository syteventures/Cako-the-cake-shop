## 1. Google Business Profile, step by step

**Before you start**
- Put the signboard up so it can be read from the road. Google often asks for a video of it.
- Use a Google account that you, the owner, control (not a staff member's). Add a second manager later.
- Search "Cako" on Google Maps first. If a listing already exists, press **Claim this business** instead of making a new one. A second profile at the same address is one of the worst things for ranking.

**Create it**
1. Go to business.google.com and add your business.
2. Business name: `Cako The Cake Shop`.
3. Primary category: **Cake shop**. This is the biggest single local ranking factor, so pick exactly this.
4. "Add a location customers can visit?" **Yes.** Type the address from section 0 and drag the pin onto your door.
5. "Do you also serve customers outside this location?" **Yes** (you deliver). Add only the areas you really deliver to. Service areas don't change ranking; they inform customers.
6. Phone: +91 84469 66779. Website: leave it empty until the real site is live.
7. Verify. Google picks the method: SMS or call, email, a recorded video, a live video call or a postcard.
   For a video, record one continuous clip: the signboard and the street or neighbouring shops, then your products and equipment (display fridge, oven, branded boxes), then something only the owner can do (opening the shutter, the billing counter, the kitchen). Google says reviews take up to 5 business days.

**Finish the profile (after verification)**
- **Hours:** Monday to Sunday, 11:00 AM to 12:00 AM. Choose "12:00 AM" as closing time and don't tick "Open 24 hours". Add Special hours before festivals if they change. Being open at the time of a search helps you show up, so keep the hours right.
- **Additional categories:** Bakery, Dessert shop, Pastry shop. Add Cupcake shop or Wedding bakery only if you sell those.
- **Description:** paste the text below.
- **Attributes:** Delivery, Takeaway, Dine-in, and the payment types you actually accept. If Google offers a vegetarian-only option, turn it on.
- **Menu and products:** real items, real photos, real prices. Not the demo menu from the website.
- **Photos:** logo (square) and cover first, then the shot list in section 3.
- **Chat:** if Edit profile > Contact offers a WhatsApp or chat link, add the same number.
- **Website (once live):** `https://YOUR-DOMAIN/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp`, so you can see which visits came from Google Maps.
- **Updates:** post one short update a week (a new cake, festival orders). Posts don't lift ranking, but customers read them.

**Description to paste (554 characters; the limit is 750)**

> Cako The Cake Shop is a vegetarian-only cake shop and bakery on Upper Kondhwa Road, Ambika Nagar, Bibvewadi, Pune. We are open every day from 11 am to 12 midnight, so there is still time to get a cake when a celebration comes up late in the evening or a birthday starts at midnight. Take your cake home, sit down and enjoy it at the shop, or order it for home delivery. Call us to ask what is ready today or to place an order. We are a neighbourhood cake shop, and we would love to be part of your next birthday, anniversary or small celebration at home.

Google rejects descriptions with links, prices or offers, so keep those out. If every cake you sell is eggless, add one sentence saying so. Never say it if any cake contains egg.

## 3. Photo shot list (for Google, Zomato and the website)

Shoot on a phone in daylight, holding it steady. Use real photos only: no stock photos, no AI pictures, no heavy filters. Google takes JPG or PNG from 10 KB to 5 MB and recommends at least 720 x 720 px.

1. The signboard from across the road, in daylight, with the name readable.
2. The same at night with the lights on. This sells "open till midnight".
3. The shop seen from Upper Kondhwa Road with a nearby landmark, so people can find you.
4. The entrance.
5. The display counter or fridge, full and lit.
6. The seating, tidy and empty.
7. Your 8 to 12 best-selling cakes: each whole from a 45° angle, plus a cut slice, on a plain background.
8. Pastries and small items lined up.
9. A cake being finished (piping, writing a name). Hands only.
10. A packed box, ready to hand over or send out.
11. The team behind the counter (only with their permission).
12. Logo (square) and a landscape cover photo.
13. One video of up to 30 seconds (75 MB or less, 720p or better): walking in from the road to the counter.

Add 3 to 5 new photos a month. Don't bother geotagging them; it has no effect. Swap the website's stand-in photos for these before launch.

## 4. Reviews: real ones, the right way

**The website's 5-star rating and reviews are made-up samples for this demo. Delete them before the site goes public.** Publishing invented reviews breaks Google's fake-engagement policy, and India's Consumer Protection Act, 2019 treats misleading claims as an unfair trade practice. BIS standard IS 19000:2022 also covers online reviews. Never put any reviews or star ratings into the website's structured data. Google doesn't show stars for a business's reviews of itself.

**How to ask**
- Get your link: Business Profile > **Ask for reviews** > copy. Turn it into a QR code for a counter card, a box sticker and the bill.
- Ask **every** customer, not only the happy ones. Asking only happy customers ("review gating") is against Google's rules.
- Ask at the counter as you hand the cake over, and on WhatsApp after a delivery:
  > Thank you for ordering from Cako The Cake Shop! How was the cake? A quick Google review helps families nearby find us: {review link}
  >
  > Hindi (Roman): Cako The Cake Shop se order karne ke liye shukriya! Cake kaisa laga? Google par ek chhota sa review likhiye, isse aas-paas ke logon ko humein dhoondhne mein madad milegi: {review link}

**Never**
- Offer a discount, free item or cashback for a review.
- Buy reviews, or ask family or staff to write them.
- Post reviews for customers, or from the shop's phone or Wi-Fi.
- Reuse the demo reviews anywhere.

**Keep it going**
- Your first target is 10 Google reviews; studies show a visible jump in ranking around there. After that, aim for a few every week. Don't let 2 to 3 weeks pass with no new review.
- Reply to every review within 2 days.
  - Good review: "Thank you, {name}! So glad you enjoyed the {cake}. See you again soon. Cako"
  - Bad review: "Sorry to hear this, {name}. Please call us on +91 84469 66779 so we can put it right." Stay calm and never argue in public.

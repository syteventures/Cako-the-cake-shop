## Product Purpose

Cako The Cake Shop is a new, vegetarian-only cake shop and bakery on Upper Kondhwa Road, Bibvewadi, Pune. It has
no website and no business profile yet. The page has to make the shop findable and believable, then turn a visit
into a call, a WhatsApp order, a Zomato order or a walk-in. Success: within seconds a visitor knows what Cako sells,
that it is open 11 am to 12 midnight every day, where it is, and how to order.

## Positioning

midnight. Vegetarian only. About ₹100 for one, so it is an everyday stop, not only an occasion shop. Delivery,
takeaway and seats on the same counter. *(the midnight angle is inferred as the differentiator; competitor hours are

## Product Principles

1. Answer "is it open, where is it, how do I order?" before anything else.
2. Phone first: one tap to call, WhatsApp or get directions, from anywhere on the page.
3. Late hours are the edge: lean on 11 am to midnight.
4. Never state an unconfirmed fact as true; demo content stays labelled as demo for the owner.
5. Light and fast on a mid-range Android phone on 4G.

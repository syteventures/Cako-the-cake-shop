How ordering works

  1. Fill in the slip. Cake, size, day, and the writing for the top.
  2. Send it on WhatsApp. Your message is typed out. You only press send.
  3. We confirm. We reply with the price and the ready time. Then you collect it, or we talk about delivery.

Pickup and delivery

Pickup

Collect from the counter at Survey 658/9, Ambika Nagar, Upper Kondhwa Road, Bibvewadi. We’re open every day, 11 am to 12 midnight. Tell us roughly when you’ll come.

Delivery

We do home delivery. Send your address on WhatsApp, and we’ll confirm whether we reach you, the charge and the time. Planning a midnight delivery? Ask us before you plan around it.

Getting it home

  1. Carry the box flat and level. In a car, the floor is flatter than a seat.
  2. On a scooter, keep the box level and take the speed breakers slowly.
  3. Keep it out of the sun.
  4. Put it in the fridge, in its box, as soon as you’re home.
  5. Take it out 15 to 20 minutes before you cut it.
  6. Cream cakes don’t like heat. Put what’s left back in the fridge soon after cutting.
  7. Ask us how long your cake keeps.

Ordering questions

Are your cakes eggless?

We’re a vegetarian-only shop. For any question about ingredients, including egg, ask us on WhatsApp before you order.

Can I get a cake for a midnight birthday?

Yes. We’re open till 12, so you can collect your cake from the counter before midnight. For delivery, ask us on WhatsApp first.

Do you deliver?

Yes, we do home delivery. Send your address on WhatsApp and we’ll confirm whether we reach you, the charge and the time.

How far ahead should I order?

It depends on the cake and the size. Send us the date on WhatsApp and we’ll tell you. For a big or custom cake, give us as much notice as you can.

Can you write a name on the cake?

Yes. Type it in the order slip exactly as it should read, up to 20 characters.

Which size should I order?

Roughly: ½ kg serves about 4 to 6 people, 1 kg about 8 to 10, 1.5 kg about 12 to 15 and 2 kg about 16 to 20. These are approximate, and thinner slices go further.

How do I pay?

Ask us on WhatsApp when you order, and we’ll tell you how.

How do I keep the cake fresh?

Keep it in the fridge, in its box, and take it out 15 to 20 minutes before you cut it. Cream cakes don’t like heat, so don’t leave yours out for long.
